🧩 Building Apps

The difference between a website and an app, databases, login, layers, and how to publish.

What it is:

A website shows content; an app stores data and responds to the user.

Why learn:

Knowing what you want keeps you from building the wrong thing.

Key concepts:

Static · interactive · state.

What it is:

The place where the app stores data permanently.

Why learn:

Without a database, everything disappears when the page closes.

Key concepts:

Table · record · persistence.

What it is:

Each user signs in with their own account and sees only what belongs to them.

Why learn:

It’s the foundation of any app with more than one person.

Key concepts:

Authentication · session · permission.

What it is:

Build the app in parts, validating each one before moving on to the next.

Why learn:

Layer by layer prevents the chaos of doing everything at once.

Key concepts:

Increment · validate · stack.

What it is:

Simulate real app usage from beginning to end.

Why learn:

Catches bugs before users do and gives you confidence to publish.

Key concepts:

Real workflow · bug · coverage.

What it is:

Put the app online with the database and login working.

Why learn:

Only once it’s live will you find out whether the app solves the problem.

Key concepts:

Deploy · production · real feedback.
