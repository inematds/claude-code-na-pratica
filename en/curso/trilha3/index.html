🤖 Always-On Personal Agent

An AI chief of staff in your pocket: a channel on your phone, connected tools, and tasks that run on their own.

What it is:

Run the agent on a machine that stays on and talk to it from your phone.

Why learn:

You call the shots from anywhere, without being tied to your desktop.

Key concepts:

Always-on · remote access · phone as the controller.

What it is:

An agent that organizes, remembers, and handles tasks for you.

Why learn:

Get out of your head what you can delegate to a machine.

Key concepts:

Delegate · personal assistant · persistent context.

What it is:

A messaging channel for talking with the agent on your phone.

Why learn:

It’s the most natural way to ask for things while on the go.

Key concepts:

Messaging · bridge · a reply in your pocket.

What it is:

Connect the agent to your calendar, email, and notes through connectors.

Why learn:

Without access to your data, the agent can only talk—it can't act.

Key concepts:

MCP · integrations · minimum permissions.

What it is:

Scheduled routines that trigger without you having to ask each time.

Why learn:

Repeated work goes on autopilot.

Key concepts:

Cron · trigger · daily summary.

What it is:

Run the agent inside a container isolated from everything else.

Why learn:

Limits the damage if something goes wrong or is malicious.

Key concepts:

Container · sandbox · minimal surface.

🛡️ Compliance & Maintenance

Red-team yourself: security, privacy, LGPD, backups, and risk checklists.

What it is:

Attack your own system before someone else does.

Why learn:

Finding issues first is cheaper than fixing them later.

Key concepts:

Red team · attack surface · realistic threat.

What it is:

The minimum rules for handling data legally and securely.

Why learn:

Fines and loss of trust are costly.

Key concepts:

LGPD · consent · data minimization.

What it is:

Separate and protect data that doesn't belong to you.

Why learn:

A customer data leak is the worst-case scenario.

Key concepts:

Segregation · restricted access · disposal.

What it is:

Automatic, tested backups.

Why learn:

A backup that has never been restored is not a backup.

Key concepts:

3-2-1 · automatic · restore test.

What it is:

How long the agent takes to respond and act.

Why learn:

Slow performance erodes trust in the system.

Key concepts:

Latency · SLA · user expectations.

What it is:

Routines and lists to keep everything healthy and up to date.

Why learn:

A system without maintenance quietly rots.

Key concepts:

Checklist · periodic review · update.

💼 Monetization

Turn your skill into revenue: find the constraint, move up the service ladder, and charge based on value.

What it is:

Find the bottleneck that most limits the client's results.

Why learn:

The money is in targeting the right constraint.

Key concepts:

Bottleneck · leverage · pain people will pay to solve.

What it is:

Sell more, deliver better, and create something new, in a cycle.

Why learn:

Gives you a clear direction for where to invest effort.

Key concepts:

More · better · new.

What it is:

Start with an audit, move on to implementation, and close with a retainer.

Why learn:

Each step builds trust and recurring revenue.

Key concepts:

Audit · project · retainer.

What it is:

Charge for the results delivered, not the hours spent.

Why learn:

AI reduces your hours — hourly pricing penalizes you for that.

Key concepts:

Value · outcome · anchoring.

What it is:

Reach out to clients in a personalized and helpful way.

Why learn:

Respect opens doors; spam burns your reputation.

Key concepts:

Research · value first · follow-up.

What it is:

Deliver the service and collect payment through a reliable payment method.

Why learn:

Without simple payment processing, the sale stalls at the end.

Key concepts:

Stripe · payment link · receipt.
